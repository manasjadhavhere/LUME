Use case: ui-mockup.
Asset type: visual direction board for LUME, an Indian makeup artist discovery and appointment request marketplace. This is a proposal, not an implemented screen.
Primary request: create a polished landscape design presentation showing one large desktop homepage concept on the left and two smaller mobile screens on the right: artist search results and an artist booking request summary.
Brand: LUME. Existing brand uses blush pink, serif editorial headlines and clean sans-serif functional text. Evolve it into warm, confident beauty editorial design with ivory #FBF7F3, deep plum #35252F, muted dusty rose #AD4968 and restrained champagne accents.
Composition: clean flat interface presentation on pale warm grey, no angled devices. Top title "LUME / THE NEXT CHAPTER". Subtitle "Beauty with clarity." Label small footer "CONCEPT • SAMPLE CONTENT".
Desktop: slim navigation LUME, Find an artist, Looks, How it works, Saved, Sign in. Split hero with clear live-style headline "Find your makeup artist." and supporting "For your occasion, your city, your style." on left, luminous natural editorial photo of an adult Indian woman with medium brown skin and refined soft-glam makeup on right. No text baked into the photograph. Prominent search control with "Occasion", "City", "Date (optional)", dark plum "Find artists". Below show four small occasion chips and three photographic artist cards, each with name, city, a sample rating and review count, service-labeled starting price in rupees. Use fictional names.
Mobile search screen: heading "Makeup artists in Mumbai", compact service/city/date bar, "Filters" and "Sort", two clear portfolio-first artist cards, no inflated badges.
Mobile request screen: adult artist portrait, "Request your date", "Party makeup", "12 Nov · 10:00", "At your venue", price estimate, short truthful note "Artist approval required. No payment now.", primary plum button "Send booking request".
Style: premium beauty magazine meets a practical appointment service, readable labels, controlled whitespace, realistic photo crops, fine quiet dividers, subtly rounded cards, no glassmorphism, no heavy gradients, no sparkles, no cartoon icons, no excessive decorative flourishes. Sample amounts and people are fictional. High design fidelity, crisp text.
